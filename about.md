## Hi, I'm Jeremy!

I'm a data professional based in Mississippi, originally from Southern California. Before moving into data, I spent a decade in Nashville pursuing music. I first got interested in data and analytics while marketing my own drum school, which sparked a new direction for my career.

During the COVID pandemic, I made the transition into data analytics. My work quickly expanded into data engineering, where I discovered a passion for building reliable data pipelines, designing data models, and developing the infrastructure that makes data useful.

That path led me to an opportunity at a startup, where I helped build its data infrastructure from the ground up. Today, I work in the utility industry and continue to grow my expertise in data engineering and analytics engineering.

Outside of data, I'm a husband, a dad of two, and a proud owner of an Aussiedoodle.
